# T30 — Respect-rules review and playtest

**Verdict: PASS with data fixes applied. No blockers. Three majors need code or art changes by the coordinator (abduction snake head reads as a haloed blue figure; `BIG_JUMP` is unreachable so `ladderBig` content never plays; Hindi bystander lines address Rama/Sita as तुम).**

Reviewed on branch `m5-polish` at 6a41c56 against the binding rules: Rama and Sita are never humiliated (no pratfalls, crying fits, slapstick; their sad is quiet disappointment; Rama congratulates when he loses); comedy comes from Hanuman and Lakshmana; snakes are Ravana's forces or story setbacks, never a deity. Audience: all ages, Indian and diaspora families.

Audited: `src/i18n/en.json` and `hi.json` (all 96 speech keys, 288 lines each), `src/characters/reactions.json` (96 entries), `src/characters/faces.ts`, `src/characters/Character.ts`, `src/core/boards.ts`, `scripts/art/snakes.mjs` (13 heads, rendered sheet in `screenshots-tmp/t30-snakes.png`), `CREDITS.md`. Playtest captures are in `screenshots-tmp/t30-*.png` (step script `screenshots-tmp/t30-steps.mjs`).

### Checked and clean

- Rama and Sita speech: no panic, complaint or gloat; sad is quiet ("A setback. Nothing more.", "A small sorrow. It will pass."); Rama's `self.lose` congratulates first ("Congratulations, my friend!").
- Rama and Sita reactions: tweens limited to nod, bless, clap, salute, sigh, leap and a soft bounce; never somersault, slump, cower, stomp or fidget; never the `scared` face.
- Faces (`faces.ts`): for Rama and Sita no tear, no sweat, no sparkles, calm brows even on `scared`. Confirmed in `t30-gallery.png`.
- Character props and costume: Rama bow and tilak, Sita flower and braid, Lakshmana bow and sash, Hanuman tail and mace. Nothing mocking.
- Board names: chronology matches Valmiki (Bala -> Ayodhya -> Aranya -> Kishkindha -> Sundara -> Yuddha; Prahasta before Kumbhakarna before the Brahmastra). No deity is a snake; Manthara and Kaikeyi are story setbacks, the rest are rakshasas or Ravana's weapons.
- Snake heads: all 13 are villains, weapons or setbacks; Kabandha is headless with chest eyes as in the epic; Kumbhakarna is asleep with a drum; Ravana has ten crowned heads.
- `CREDITS.md`: fonts (Yatra One, Noto Sans, Noto Sans Devanagari) are OFL on Google Fonts; package versions match `package.json`; art is procedural with no third-party images; licence matches `LICENSE`.
